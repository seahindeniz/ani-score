# {frontmatter.title}

AniScore works on any anime site once it knows where the site's episode cards are. You can teach it a new site yourself, right on the site, without writing code, and share it so AniScore works there for everyone.

## Turn on a built-in site

Some sites come with AniScore but stay off until you turn them on: open AniScore's side panel and choose the site under **Add a site**. Your browser asks whether AniScore may access that site; it never gets access to sites you didn't turn on. **Turn off** in the **On** list turns it off again.

## Integrate a new site

1. Open a page of the site that lists episodes, such as its latest episodes.
2. Right-click the page (or AniScore's toolbar button) and choose **Integrate this site with AniScore**. Allow access when your browser asks, so AniScore keeps working there after you save.
3. A setup panel opens and asks for one thing at a time, mostly a click on the page:
   1. **Find the episode cards**: click any episode. AniScore outlines the cards like it; use **Make bigger** if the outline only covers part of a card.
   2. **Pick the anime's name** in one of the cards.
   3. **Pick the episode number**. AniScore keeps only the number, and reads the season too when the cards show one, like _2. Sezon 5. Bölüm_. On a site without episode numbers, choose **No episodes here**.
   4. **Choose where the score goes**. AniScore's details now show on the page, so you see the result right away.
   5. **Match the site's colors** (optional): dark, light, or the site's own light and dark switch, which AniScore learns when you flip it once.
   6. **Highlight anime on your list** (optional): the part of a card that glows while the anime is on your AniList list, and the space between the cards so the glow doesn't run into the next one.
   7. **Check and save**: review what AniScore reads, choose whether scores show on pages like this one or on every page of the site, and click **Save**.

You can drag the panel by its header. Your work is kept as a draft if you close it before saving.

## Change a site's setup

Choose **Integrate this site with AniScore** again on a site that already has a setup, and AniScore asks whether to **Edit the setup** or **Start from scratch**. The editor lists the same steps, each with what it reads now, and marks the ones that need a look. Open one to change it. Its **Advanced** settings hold the details behind it: selectors, the rules that read a value (with ready-made ones like _"12. Bölüm" → 12_), fallbacks, CSS and the page addresses.

To set up another page of the site, choose **Add a page** in the editor's page list. For a page whose address doesn't change, like a single-page app that pages in place, set **Only while this exists** in the page's advanced settings. For a page with a second kind of card, use **Add another kind of card**.

## Share it with everyone

After saving, in the editor's menu, or next to your site in the side panel, click **Share**. It opens a GitHub issue with your setup filled in. A maintainer reviews it, and once it's approved it ships with the next AniScore release, for everyone to turn on.

## Remove a site

Choose **Delete this setup** in the editor's menu, or use the side panel: **Export** downloads a setup, **Import a config** adds one, and **Delete** (or **Reset to built-in** for a built-in site you edited) removes yours.
